Gesture tuning · prototype

Throwaway. Tap a Pin to remove it, drag to turn the car, press and hold a Part to Peek, pinch or scroll to zoom. There are no Boxes or Buffer: the top bands only reserve their space so the car is its real size, and they show a live gesture log. Every value below is a developer constant. Changes apply at once and are saved in the URL.

What to try on the phone

  • Taps. Tap quickly, and tap while walking. Does every tap you mean count, and does no small drag count as a tap? The log shows each tap's time and how far your finger moved.
  • Small Pins. At 1× the Pin heads are only about 17 px. Turn on Aim test and take about 30 taps. It shows how far off-centre your taps land, so you can see what target size you need.
  • Close Pins. Try the three engine Pins (lift the bonnet first) and the two spare-wheel Pins at the back. Does the right one come off?
  • Blocked Pins. Tap a seat Pin through the window: it should wiggle and its door should flash.
  • Peek. Hold a door or the bonnet. Is the wait right, so it doesn't fire when you tap slowly? While Peeking, drag to turn the car, then let go.
  • Two fingers. Start a hold or a drag, then put a second finger down. Then lift one finger of a pinch before the other.
  • Desktop. Click, drag, hold, scroll, and pinch on a trackpad.
