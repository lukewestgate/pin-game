What to judge

  • Car: Lambert with no outlines (A), soft toon with outlines on the Pins only (B), or toon with ink outlines everywhere (C)? Do the bevels make it read as a toy?
  • Pin head: domed flush disc, taller pan head, or the old tapered cylinder? Is the cross recess still readable at 1× on the 360-wide screen? Turn on the colour-blind setting: can you tell the drives apart?
  • Boxes: tinted rim around a white panel (A), solid tin (B) or flat sticker (C)? Does an empty slot read as a hole?
  • Buffer warning: drag “Buffer filled” to 5. Slots warming plus a pulse (A), amber rings then a red pulse (B), or a counter badge that shakes (C)?
  • Background: pale gradient, dark slate vignette, or warm stripes? Try Landscape: it fills the letterbox.
  • Buttons, type, banners: candy, pill or sticker? Bounce-in card, ribbon, or slapped sticker?
  • Colour-blind switch: its own button in the bar, or a small settings sheet behind one button (with Sound)?
