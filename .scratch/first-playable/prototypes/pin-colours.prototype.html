Protanopia and deuteranopia (red-green) affect about 1 in 12 men together; tritanopia (blue-yellow) is rare. "Lightness only" shows what survives with no hue at all, a good worst case.

How far apart the colours are

Smallest OKLab distance ×100 between any two Pin colours in play, and from any Pin colour to the car's body colours (cream, greys, tyre). Rough guide:  ≥ 12 clear   7–12 look twice   < 7 confusable  at Pin size.

What to judge

  • With 5 colours and normal vision: do the Pins pop off the cream and grey car, and do they look like a set you'd want in the game?
  • Switch the vision to protan and deutan: can you still sort every Pin in the Buffer to its Box at a glance?
  • Cue A vs B vs C vs D: which second cue (if any) helps without cluttering? Does it still read at 1× zoom on the smallest phone?
  • Push the colour count to 8 and 10 (Endless hardness): where does colour alone stop working, and does the cue carry it?
  • Lambert vs cel shading: does the shadowed side of a Pin change its colour enough to confuse it with a neighbour?
