Prototype: how one tap's animations play out

One tap can set off a chain: the Pin flies to a Box or the Buffer, its Part may fall away, a full Box closes, a new one slides in, and matching Buffer Pins drain into it, sometimes closing that Box too. This prototype lets you feel different answers to four questions:

1. Does the chain play strictly one step at a time, or do steps overlap? 2. How long is each step, and how far apart do draining Pins leave? 3. What happens if you tap again before the chain finishes (ignored, queued, or played at once)? 4. What does Undo do to a chain that's still playing?

Drag to turn the car. Tap a Pin to remove it. The rules always decide the outcome straight away; only the animations wait. Shortcuts, not decisions: no zoom or Peek here, and the Box row and Buffer are fixed objects in the same scene instead of a second camera.
