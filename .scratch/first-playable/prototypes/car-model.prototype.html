What to judge

  • Does it read as a toy? Puffy moulded vinyl (A), toy bricks (B) or a painted wooden toy (C)? Compare with the current car (0).
  • Do the Pins still pop? The car should stay quieter than the Pins. Check the plate tints in A and C against the red, pink and blue Pins.
  • Can you see the inner Parts? Remove the outer Parts: the axles, engine, seats, steering wheel and rear seat should read clearly, with their Pins easy to tap.
  • The win state: strip to the Cores. Is the leftover chassis a nice thing to end on?
  • Backdrop: the settled dark slate, or the inspiration's pale sky? Toon or soft shading? (These are only here to check the car against; UI visual style settled them.)
