What to judge

  • On the smallest phone (360 × 640): are the Box and Buffer Pins big enough to read colours at a glance, and is the car still big enough to aim at?
  • Boxes and Buffer: together at the top, split around the car, or together in a bottom tray?
  • Do the safe areas (notch, home bar) stay clear? Try iPhone 14 with “Show regions”.
  • Landscape and desktop: centred portrait column, or spread out with the car on the left?
  • Win and lose banners, Restart, and the dev-only Undo: placement and size.
  • Try Boxes 4, Buffer 8 or Capacity 4 to see the layout reflow.
